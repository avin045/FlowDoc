FlowDoc 1.1.0 records browser tab switches and ignores clicks that change nothing. The recorder bar stays out of your screenshots, and guides copy as Markdown.

**Install:** download `FlowDoc.Setup.1.1.0.exe` below and run it. It installs over 1.0.0 and keeps your guides and settings.

Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" the first time, because the installer isn't code-signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## What's new

### Switching browser tabs becomes a "Navigate to" step
- These actions add a **Navigate to &lt;link&gt;** step, with the page's address and a screenshot of it:
  - switching to another browser tab;
  - typing an address and pressing Enter.
- Switching back to the page you were already on adds nothing.
- **Desktop app (Windows):** works when you click a tab, use Ctrl+Tab, Ctrl+PageUp/PageDown or Ctrl+1–9, or press Enter in the address bar. It works in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave, Vivaldi and Opera. On Linux a tab click stays a normal click.
- **Extension:** works when you switch tabs or browser windows.

### Repeated clicks that change nothing are ignored
People often click again when an app is slow, or when they aren't sure a click worked. FlowDoc no longer turns those repeats into extra steps.
- A click on the same thing again is dropped when it changed nothing on screen. It is also dropped when nothing had changed since the previous click.
- Clicks, double-clicks and right-clicks are each compared with the same kind of click. A click followed by a double-click stays two steps.
- Clicks that visibly change something, even a number going from 1 to 2, are always kept.
- To record every click, untick **Settings → Capture → Ignore repeated clicks that change nothing**. It's on by default, in both the desktop app and the extension.

### Copy a guide as text or Markdown
The guide's **Copy** button is now a menu:
- **Text**
- **Markdown with images**: the screenshots are embedded in the text, so pasting into Obsidian and other Markdown apps keeps them.
- **Markdown without images**

### The recorder bar stays out of your screenshots
When **Hide the recorder bar from screen recordings and meetings** is unticked, FlowDoc's own screenshots now show what's behind the bar instead of a blurred patch. Meeting and screen-recording apps still see the bar; it blinks out for a moment at each click. On Linux the bar is still blurred.

## Fixes
- **Extension:** a double-click is no longer sometimes recorded as a plain "Click".
- **Desktop app:** clicking the browser tab that's already showing no longer adds a "Click" step.

See the [user guide](https://github.com/avin045/FlowDoc/blob/main/docs/user-guide.md) for how to record, edit and export guides.
