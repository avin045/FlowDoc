FlowDoc 1.1.2 keeps passwords out of every screenshot, brings Save to Notion to the Chrome / Edge extension, encrypts the extension's keys, and makes desktop guides much smaller and quicker to save.

**Install:** download `FlowDoc.Setup.1.1.2.exe` below and run it. It installs over 1.1.1 and keeps your guides and settings. On first start, FlowDoc moves the screenshots of your existing guides into separate image files in the background; your guides stay as they are.

Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" the first time, because the installer isn't code-signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. See [If your browser or Windows warns you](https://github.com/avin045/FlowDoc/blob/main/docs/installation.md#if-your-browser-or-windows-warns-you).

**Extension:** update it as described in [Update the extension](https://github.com/avin045/FlowDoc/blob/main/docs/installation.md#update-the-extension): unzip over the same folder and click reload, and your guides and keys are kept.

## What's new

### Passwords stay out of screenshots
Before, only the step text hid passwords. A screenshot could still show a password field's dots, or the password itself once you clicked **Show password**. Now, in both apps:
- Every password field on screen is covered with one solid colour in the screenshot before it's stored. No characters, dots or length show.
- This still works after **Show password**: a field that was a password field earlier in the capture stays one, and text typed into it afterwards isn't recorded.
- Fields labelled as passwords or PINs are covered too, in many languages, Tamil and Hindi included.
- **Extension:** password fields inside web components and same-site frames are found and covered as well.
- If a password field can't be covered, the step keeps no screenshot at all, rather than one that might show it.

What it can't see yet is listed under [Privacy and passwords](https://github.com/avin045/FlowDoc/blob/main/docs/user-guide.md#privacy-and-passwords).

### Save to Notion in the extension
The extension now saves guides to Notion the same way as the desktop app:
- the same page picker: browse pages and subpages like folders, or search;
- **Recent**, up to 10 named saved locations, and **Replace the copy saved on …**;
- screenshots uploaded, and **Open in Notion**, also later from the guide's **⋯** menu.

Connect it in **Settings → Integrations**. See [Save guides to Notion](https://github.com/avin045/FlowDoc/blob/main/docs/user-guide.md#save-guides-to-notion-optional).

### The extension encrypts your keys
The AI key and the Notion secret are stored encrypted with AES-GCM. The key comes from the browser itself, which keeps it apart from the encrypted keys and never lets any script read it out. Anyone looking at the extension's storage sees only random bytes. Keys saved by 1.1.1 are encrypted automatically the first time the workspace opens, so you don't need to paste them again.

### Smaller, faster guides in the desktop app
- Screenshots are saved once as image files next to your guides, so a guide file stays a few KB and saving a step stays quick, even in long guides.
- Captures are stored as WebP, which takes less space.
- The editor loads screenshots as you scroll to them.
- Moving your guides folder takes the images along, and a **Backup (.json)** still contains everything.

### Extension: only active while capturing
The recorder runs on a page only while you capture, instead of on every page you visit.

## Security
- Imported guide files are cleaned before they open: script links and remote images are dropped.
- Step links reach the editor and exports only as `http`, `https` or `file` links.
- The workspace has a Content Security Policy. In the desktop app the page can't reach the network at all.
- **Desktop app:**
  - FlowDoc's windows can't be sent to other websites; web links open in your browser.
  - Permissions FlowDoc doesn't need are refused.
  - The installer is hardened: it can't be run as Node.js or with a debugger attached, and it checks its own files when it starts.
- **Linux without GNOME Keyring or KWallet:** Settings now says that keys are only obfuscated, not encrypted.
- Found a security problem? Report it privately, as described in [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/avin045/FlowDoc/blob/main/SECURITY.md).
