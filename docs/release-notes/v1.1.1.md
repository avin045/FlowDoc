FlowDoc 1.1.1 puts Settings into a sidebar of sections, stops repeated double-clicks and tooltip clicks from adding extra steps, and keeps the recorder bar out of every screenshot.

**Install:** download `FlowDoc.Setup.1.1.1.exe` below and run it. It installs over 1.1.0 and keeps your guides and settings.

Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" the first time, because the installer isn't code-signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## What's new

### Settings in a sidebar
Settings used to be one long scrolling page. Its sections are now in a sidebar on the left, and only the section you pick is shown:
- **General:** click highlight style and the capture shortcut.
- **Capture:** minimising FlowDoc, the recorder bar, typed text and repeated clicks.
- **Storage:** the guides folder.
- **AI:** the AI assistant (Gemini or Groq).
- **Integrations:** Notion.

Details:
- Settings opens again on the section you used last. **Open AI settings** goes straight to **AI**.
- The dialog keeps the same size for every section, so it doesn't jump when you switch.
- With the keyboard, Up and Down move between sections, and Home and End jump to the first and last.
- In a narrow window, the sections become a row of tabs above the settings.
- The browser extension gets the same sidebar, with General, Capture and AI.

## Fixes

### Double-clicking the same word again no longer adds steps
Double-clicking a word again (for example in Chrome, with the **Define** bubble showing) used to add a "Double-click" step every time. Two things made those repeats count as changes:
- the bubble moves a little with where exactly you clicked, and fades away;
- the second double-click sometimes selected the word with the space after it.

Now:
- What comes and goes just below the pointer, such as tooltips and selection bubbles, doesn't count as a change.
- For a double-click, re-selecting the same word on its line doesn't count either.
- Clicking a button again while its tooltip comes and goes is no longer a new step.
- Changes beside the pointer still count, so a number going from 1 to 2 next to a button keeps the step.

To record every click anyway, untick **Settings → Capture → Ignore repeated clicks that change nothing**.

### The recorder bar no longer slips into a screenshot
This applies when **Hide the recorder bar from screen recordings and meetings** is unticked. If two screenshots were taken at almost the same moment, for example while FlowDoc was judging a repeated click, the bar could appear in one of them. It now stays out of every shot.
